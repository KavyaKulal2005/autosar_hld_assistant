DOCUMENT: HLD-01
TITLE: Synthetic Vehicle Speed and Braking Architecture
REVISION: A
SECTION: Overview
This illustrative high-level design describes a synthetic vehicle software architecture.
The SensorManager receives speed measurements from SpeedSensor and publishes the VehicleSpeed signal.
The SensorManager provides the SpeedData interface to the VehicleGateway component.

SECTION: Braking
The BrakeController consumes VehicleSpeed through the SpeedData interface.
The BrakeController publishes the BrakeStatus signal through the BrakeStatusPort interface.
The BrakeModule provides braking status to the BrakeController.

SECTION: Gateway
The VehicleGateway forwards selected vehicle signals to the BodyControl component.
The VehicleGateway uses the GatewayInput interface.
The BodyControl component consumes DoorStatus and publishes CabinLightCommand.

SECTION: Notes
This sample is synthetic for educational demonstration. It is not a real vehicle design or AUTOSAR specification.
