TITLE: Synthetic Architecture SYN-TEST-05
REVISION: A
SECTION: Overview
The LightingCoordinator receives CruiseSpeed from WiperCoordinator.
LightingDriver and WiperCoordinator exchange ParkingPosition.
LightingDriver and LightingCoordinator exchange CruiseState.
Via CruiseDataHook, the LightingCoordinator distributes ParkingPosition.
ParkingPosition is emitted by the LightingCoordinator on CruiseDataHook.
The WiperCoordinator consumes ParkingPosition.
The ChargingDriver exposes WiperTorqueInfo for CruiseSpeed exchange.
Via PedalStatusPort, the WiperCoordinator distributes PedalPosition.
Access to CruiseSpeed is granted through WiperTorqueInfo.
WiperDataChannel carries CruiseSpeed from the CruiseSupervisor.
The ChargingDriver subscribes to CruiseState from LightingMonitor.
LightingCoordinator and LightingDriver exchange CruiseState.
CruiseState is emitted by the ChargingDriver on WiperDataChannel.
Via PedalStatusPort, the WiperCoordinator distributes PedalPosition.
The LightingMonitor subscribes to CruiseState from LightingCoordinator.
The WiperCoordinator receives CruiseSpeed from CruiseSupervisor.
The LightingMonitor subscribes to CruiseState from CruiseSupervisor.
The WiperCoordinator subscribes to PedalPosition from LightingDriver.
Access to CruiseState is granted through WiperTorqueInfo.
Via PedalStatusPort, the LightingCoordinator distributes CruiseState.
