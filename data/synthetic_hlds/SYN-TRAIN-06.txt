The BodyMonitor component depends on BodyController for WheelAngle.
The BodyController component depends on GatewayMonitor for CabinAngle.
The BodyMonitor receives WheelAngle from BrakeManager.
The BodyMonitor component depends on BodyController for BatteryHealth.
The BrakeManager provides the NetworkModeInterface interface to the NetworkModule component.
The BodyController exposes DoorModeInfo for BatteryHealth exchange.
The GatewayMonitor provides the DoorModeInfo interface to the BrakeManager component.
The BodyMonitor publishes BatteryHealth.
The GatewayMonitor uses DoorModeInfo.
EngineCmdInput carries WheelAngle from the NetworkModule.
The NetworkModule publishes CabinAngle.
The GatewayController consumes CabinAngle through the NetworkModeInterface interface.
The BodyController forwards selected signals to the BodyMonitor component.
The BodyController consumes CabinAngle.
The BrakeManager publishes the CabinAngle signal via the EngineCmdInput port.
The GatewayMonitor consumes SteeringVoltage through the NetworkModeService interface.
The NetworkModule uses NetworkModeService.
EngineCmdInput carries BatteryHealth from the BodyMonitor.
