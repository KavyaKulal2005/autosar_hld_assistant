TITLE: Synthetic Architecture SYN-TRAIN-10
REVISION: A
SECTION: Overview
The SensorManager provides the ClimateStateApi interface to the EngineMonitor component.
The SteeringModule consumes GearTorque.
The EngineHandler consumes BrakeVoltage.
The EngineMonitor uses SensorModeInfo.
The EngineHandler component depends on SteeringModule for GearTorque.
The EngineMonitor component depends on EngineHandler for DoorTemperature.
The EngineMonitor consumes DoorTemperature through the ClimateStateApi interface.
The EngineMonitor publishes GearTorque.
The SteeringModule publishes GearTorque through EngineFeedOutput.
The EngineHandler publishes the WheelHealth signal via the EngineFeedOutput port.
The EngineHandler uses SensorModeInfo.
BatteryDataLine carries CabinHealth from the EngineHandler.
The EngineHandler sends CabinHealth via BatteryDataLine.
The SteeringModule publishes the WheelHealth signal via the BatteryDataLine port.
See the SystemOverview for further details.
