The BodyHandler uses BodyTorqueInterface.
The EngineHandler uses BodyTorqueInterface.

The ClimateMonitor publishes the CabinCommand signal via the BrakeFeedOutput port.

The EngineHandler provides the GatewaySpeedApi interface to the ClimateMonitor component.

The EngineHandler publishes DoorAngle through BatteryFeedInput.
The ClimateMonitor receives CabinAngle from EngineHandler.
The DoorService consumes DoorAngle through the GatewaySpeedApi interface.
The ClimateMonitor exposes BodyTorqueInterface for DoorAngle exchange.
The DoorService publishes EngineVoltage.
The ClimateMonitor publishes DoorAngle.
The ClimateMonitor provides the GatewaySpeedApi interface to the DoorService component.
The ClimateMonitor provides the BodyTorqueInterface interface to the BodyHandler component.
The ClimateMonitor publishes the DoorAngle signal via the BatteryFeedInput port.
